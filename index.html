The Reality

By the time you
see the signs, the
campaign has already started.

Union organizing doesn't begin with a card drive. It begins months — sometimes years — earlier, in the unresolved friction between employees and the organization they work for. Unanswered compensation questions. Leadership gaps on the frontline. Communication that flows only one direction.

Most companies engage outside help after an election petition is filed. At that point, you're playing defense on someone else's timeline, and the cost — operational, financial, and cultural — is already accumulating.

We engage earlier. We go deeper. And we fix what's actually driving dissatisfaction.

"The most expensive labor crisis is the one you could have prevented three years ago."

— NLRG Advisory Principle
Our Approach

We don't just manage
labor risk.
We diagnose it.

Traditional labor consultants focus on response — legal strategy, campaign messaging, election mechanics. We start earlier and go deeper.

Our team combines decades of labor relations expertise with rigorous operational analysis. We examine the actual conditions in your workplace: management effectiveness, compensation equity, communication practices, frontline culture. We identify what drives dissatisfaction before it becomes organizing activity.

The result isn't just a won election — it's a workplace where employees have less reason to seek outside representation in the first place. A direct relationship between management and workforce, without a third party that profits from their adversarial dynamic.

The Team

We know how
organizing campaigns
are actually run.

National Labor Relations Group was built with one standard: assemble the best performers in the country and give them the resources to do their best work.

Several members of our team spent years as senior union officials. They didn't leave the labor movement because they were ineffective — they left because they concluded that employees are better served by a strong direct relationship with management than by an institution that benefits from maintaining conflict.

That inside perspective informs everything we do. We understand every tactic, every pressure point, every communication strategy an organizing campaign will deploy — because some of us deployed them.
